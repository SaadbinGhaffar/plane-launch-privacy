Privacy Policy

Plane Launch: Fly & Upgrade

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Plane Launch: Fly & Upgrade ("Plane Launch", "the game", Android package com.fathomstack.planelaunch) is published by FathomStack ("we"). This policy explains what data the game uses and why.

Game data

Your game progress (stage, coins, gems, upgrades, unlocked planes, liveries and pilots, and settings) is stored on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

The game has no accounts and no online leaderboard: your progress is never sent to our servers. We do not collect your real name, email or contacts.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game removes all data stored on your device. Advertising data is controlled by Google; you can reset your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Advertising

Plane Launch is free and is funded by ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version),
  • ad interactions (ads viewed and tapped) and app diagnostics.

This data is processed by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. Learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required): the first time you open the game you are asked whether ads may be personalised. You can change your choice at any time in the game: tap the Settings gear and choose Privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). Without it you still see ads, but they are not personalised.
  • Watching rewarded video ads is always optional.

Children

Plane Launch is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
